Global Metallurgical Technology Partnerships

While we supply technology from our global partners, Proventus Valuetech takes full ownership and responsibility for plant performance as per agreed terms and conditions.

  • Patented Core Metallurgy: Access state-of-the-art pyrometallurgical furnace configurations, advanced TBRC smelters, and energy-efficient electrowinning cell houses.
  • Performance Accountability: We guarantee specific recovery yields, energy consumption per ton, and commercial purity standards (up to LME Grade-A).
  • Technology Adaptation: Customizing international technologies to suit local feedstocks, secondary scraps, and environmental regulations.
  • Long-Term Technical Support: Continuous technology transfer, operational audits, and process tuning throughout plant life.
