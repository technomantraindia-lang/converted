About Company

Proventus Valuetech

Proventus Valuetech is a company that has expertise in processing of base metals like Cu, Zn, Pb, Co and Ni from ore or secondary scrap/waste generated of mineral/metal processing industries. We are able to provide end to end solution for setting up new plants, upgradation/modernization of existing plant, process and efficiency improvements in existing process plant. Our expertise includes consulting and providing solution for Mineral Processing, Hydrometallurgy and Pyrometallurgy of base metals.

Our core capabilities include Process Innovation, Consultancy Services, Prefeasibility and Feasibility Studies, Basic and Detailed Engineering Packages, Technical Audits, Turnkey Supply/OEC projects Â of Metallurgical Plants for base metals.

Our Vision

To be able to provide single point solution for small to medium scale plant setups so as to extract maximum value for money for the investors.

Our Mission
