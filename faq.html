Frequently Asked Questions

What metals and feedstocks does Proventus Valuetech specialize in?

We specialize in pyrometallurgical, hydrometallurgical, and electrorefining processing for base metals including Copper (Cu), Zinc (Zn), Lead (Pb), Cobalt (Co), and Nickel (Ni), from primary concentrates as well as secondary recycled materials (e-scrap, copper slags, and industrial metallic wastes).

What is the difference between Turnkey Supply and Owner's Engineering (OEC)?

Turnkey Plant Supply offers single-point accountability for design, fabrication, supply, erection, and commissioning to guarantee performance. In Owner's Engineering Consultant (OEC) mode, we act as the client's independent on-site technical authority to audit and supervise external engineering and construction contractors.

How do you ensure client intellectual property and data privacy?

All client discussions, ore grade assays, feasibility data, and plant documentation are protected under strict corporate Non-Disclosure Protocols (NDA).
