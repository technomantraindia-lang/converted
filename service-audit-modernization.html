Performance Optimization

Process Audit & Performance Modernization

Unlocking Latent Capacity and Efficiency in Existing Metallurgical Assets

If your plant is failing to hit rated capacity, consuming excessive energy per ton, or producing sub-standard cathode/product purity, you have "value leakage" within your process flow. Proventus Valuetech conducts deep-dive forensic audits of pyrometallurgical and electrorefining circuits to pinpoint bottlenecks and implement targeted engineering upgrades that restore performance and maximize profit margins.

Our Core Diagnostic & Improvement Capabilities

Forensic Process Audits

We move beyond superficial checks to perform a rigorous technical examination of your existing metallurgical circuit.

  • Mass & Energy Balance Reconciliation: Verifying if actual output aligns with theoretical potential to stop metal loss in slags and slimes.
  • Performance Benchmarking: Comparative analysis of thermal/electrochemical performance against industry-best standards.
  • Equipment Health Assessment: Evaluation of existing furnace linings, cell integrity, and material handling systems.

Modernization Upgrades

We upgrade legacy circuits by integrating modern chemical and process controls without a full plant rebuild.

  • Circuit Optimization: Redesigning thermal and chemical loops to improve kinetics and reduce smelter fuel consumption.
  • Electrorefining Tuning: Adjusting current densities, additive regimes, and electrolyte flow dynamics to eliminate contamination.
  • Metal Recovery Enhancement: Implementing specialized slag-treatment and electrolytic recovery methods.

Throughput Debottlenecking

We specialize in "sweating the assets"—increasing plant throughput (TPA) by identifying and eliminating singular constraints.

  • Flowsheet Retuning: Adjusting operational parameters to accelerate batch cycles or continuous throughput.
  • Process Control Upgrades: Integrating modern instrumentation for real-time adjustments to chemistry and power profiles.
  • Operational Standard Operating Procedures: SOP training to ensure performance gains are sustained long-term.

The Improvement Framework

Available under both Turnkey Implementation (where we execute the upgrade) and Owner’s Engineering Consultant (OEC) modes.

Phase 1

Forensic Audit & Baselining

We map your existing process, conduct forensic data analysis, and define the performance gap between current output and target capacity.

Phase 2

Engineered Solutions

We provide the exact technical roadmap—mechanical modifications, chemical dosing adjustments, or equipment tuning—to close the gap.

Phase 3

Performance Validation

We oversee implementation and conduct post-audit validation runs to guarantee the verified increase in yield, purity, or throughput.
